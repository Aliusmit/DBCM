Don Bosco College, Maram

Autonomous NAAC A+

Affiliated to Manipur University · Estd. 2000 · Salesians of Don Bosco

Don Bosco College Maram (DBCM) is a premier higher education institution situated in Senapati District, Manipur. Founded on the educational pedagogy of St. John Bosco, DBCM blends rigorous academic learning with character formation, discipline, and community leadership. Offering undergraduate degree programs across 14 diverse departments under the National Education Policy (NEP) framework with specialized Major and Minor degree paths.
